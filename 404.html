N° M-01 — Mind · Michael Sugrue

Marcus Aurelius' Meditations: The Stoic Ideal

Core ideas

  • Meditations was Marcus Aurelius' private journal, written to train his own mind while running an empire.
  • Stoicism separates what is in your control (judgment, action) from what is not (outcomes, other people).
  • Duty, reason and acceptance of impermanence are the backbone of a good life.

Why it matters

I come back to this when I catch myself reacting to things I can't change. If an emperor could journal his way back to composure, I can too.

N° M-02 — Mind · The Diary Of A CEO

Andrew Huberman: You Must Control Your Dopamine! The Shocking Truth Behind Cold Showers!

Core ideas

  • Dopamine drives motivation and pursuit, not just pleasure.
  • Constant easy hits (phones, junk, stimulation) raise your baseline and make normal life feel flat.
  • Deliberate discomfort like cold exposure can raise dopamine in a slower, sustained way.

Why it matters

This reframed discipline for me as chemistry I can manage, not willpower I either have or don't.

N° B-03 — Body · Goobie and Doobie

Help Your Body Heal Ep. 2 Part Two - How To Safely Decrease Excess Sodium Stores In Your Body

Core ideas

  • Excess sodium affects fluid balance and blood pressure.
  • Potassium-rich whole foods, hydration and cutting processed foods help the body rebalance.
  • Changes should be gradual and done with medical guidance if you have a condition.

Why it matters

Caring for the body starts with basics like this. Small, boring, consistent changes beat dramatic ones.
